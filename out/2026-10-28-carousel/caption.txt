RKC Deep 6. One side, no putting the bell down, then the other.

5 half snatches
5 single-arm swings
5 clean + press
5 racked squats
5 Turkish get-ups
Switch sides. Rest 1–2 min. 3–5 rounds.

The get-ups at the end are where the grip goes. Go lighter than you think and keep it clean.
