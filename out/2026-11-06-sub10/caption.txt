10-minute EMOM. One medium bell, start the clock.

Odd minutes: 12 swings
Even minutes: 8 goblet squats + 4 push-ups
Rest whatever's left of the minute.

If you're done in 25 seconds, the bell is too light. If you're not done by 50, it's too heavy.
